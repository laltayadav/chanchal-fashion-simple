## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Public trust information pages
The system SHALL provide public pages for contact/help, shipping, returns/exchange, privacy policy, and terms, with clear operational guidance for customers.

#### Scenario: Trust pages are navigable
- **WHEN** a customer browses the storefront
- **THEN** trust pages are reachable from visible public navigation or footer links

#### Scenario: Policy content is explicit
- **WHEN** a customer opens a policy page
- **THEN** it includes concrete, readable details about response channels, order handling, and policy expectations

### Requirement: In-flow trust messaging for ordering
The system SHALL show concise trust cues in storefront/cart flow using the existing design language.

#### Scenario: Customer sees order confidence cues
- **WHEN** a customer is evaluating or submitting an order
- **THEN** the UI shows concise trust messaging about support channel, order confirmation process, and next steps
